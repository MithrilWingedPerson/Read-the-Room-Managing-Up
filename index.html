QUICK GUIDE

Read the clues. Don't jump to conclusions.

Use observable information to decide how to approach a conversation. A manager's style is a clue, not a fixed label.

01

Notice the situation

What is happening? What is urgent? Who is affected? What information is confirmed?

02

Read the clues

Consider communication preferences, availability, knowledge, priorities and receptiveness.

03

Choose your approach

Decide what needs to be clarified, communicated, negotiated or escalated.

04

Adapt when needed

Notice the response. If your first approach isn't working, consider what you can reasonably change.

KEEP IN MIND

You cannot control how your manager responds, but you can make informed decisions about how you approach, manage and navigate the working relationship.
